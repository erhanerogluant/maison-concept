01The house

We cook to the rhythm of the year — a small dining room, a single menu, and the patience to let ingredients speak plainly.

Maison AUREL is conceived as an intimate room in Berlin for contemporary European cooking. Each evening follows one tasting menu, composed from the season and refined until nothing on the plate is there by accident.

Technique is there to serve flavour, never to be admired for its own sake. What remains is quiet, precise, and meant to be remembered.

03The kitchen

Craft, held to the season.

The kitchen works in small, deliberate steps: vegetables roasted in their skins, broths drawn over a day, fruit preserved at its peak to carry the menu into winter.

Every course is tasted, questioned and simplified. If an element does not make the dish clearer, it leaves the plate.

A profile of the head chef and kitchen team will be published here once confirmed.

04The restaurant

A room for slow evenings.

See the restaurant

Wood, lime-washed walls and linen. Light kept low and close to the table, so the evening gathers around the plate and the people you share it with.

05The evening

How an evening unfolds.

  1. 01

    Arrival

    An unhurried welcome, a seat at a table set only for you, and a first glass while the kitchen begins.

  2. 02

    The menu

    One tasting menu each evening, written around what the season offers. A vegetarian menu runs alongside it.

  3. 03

    The pace

    Courses arrive in their own time. An evening with us typically unfolds over around three hours.

  4. 04

    The pairing

    Wines and considered non-alcoholic pairings, chosen to follow the menu rather than lead it.
